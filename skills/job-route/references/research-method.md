# Research method / 来源与证据核验

## Source hierarchy
1. Current laws, regulations, official promulgation texts and relevant institution pages.
2. Current recruitment bulletins and qualification announcements, with year, locality and role.
3. Primary institutional explanations and official job descriptions.
4. Published biographies and credible secondary reporting — only to illustrate actual examples, not universal rules.

Check `published`, **effective date** (may differ), updates and superseding texts. Search at least one alternative name and one recent update. Don't rely solely on search snippets. Read the relevant provision. Distinguish nationally applicable law from provincial or employer-specific rules.

## Evidence ledger
For every disputed or concrete career claim record:
- Claim / role ID / edge IDs
- Exact authoritative source URL and publisher
- Publication date / effective scope / last verified date
- Evidence type: normative, official_description, illustrative, conditional
- Known limitations and alternative interpretations

Use IDs like `civil_law`, `court_rules`, never fake links or attach a source that does not support the claim. Where evidence conflicts, note the newer and higher-authority rule and explain uncertainties.

## Career-specific checks
- Military: military office vs rank, accession channel vs later appointment; only public non-sensitive rules.
- Judicial police: official civil-service police vs auxiliary status; police rank vs leadership appointment; differing court/procuratorate systems.
- Government: appointment recommendation, civil-service leadership grade and legal election are different events; don't claim elected office is received via exam alone.
- Global extension: identify regulator, licensing and regional differences before reusing China's categories.

## Publication threshold
No unsourced binding eligibility; no invented probabilities, age milestones or typical durations; no graph edge implying guaranteed promotion. If verification is incomplete, keep draft only.
